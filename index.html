02 — Why now

The board deck is the recurring fire drill nobody fixed.

Finance teams spend ~85% of the cycle on assembly and ~15% on insight. The numbers live across Stripe, the CRM, the cap table, and six spreadsheet tabs. So the deck eats days — and it's already stale by the time the meeting starts.

Spreadsheet sprawl

Three days, six tabs, four systems.

Pull ARR from Stripe, pipeline from the CRM, burn from the model. Reconcile it. Rebuild the same charts. Rewrite the same variance story. Every single month.

The stale deck

Built Monday. Outdated by Thursday.

A number changes after you've exported it to slides, and now you're hand-patching figures across twelve pages the night before the board meeting. Static decks go cold the moment you build them.

Wrong-number dread

One broken formula. In front of the board.

A pasted-in figure nobody can trace. A board member asks where it came from and you're guessing live. The most expensive number in the deck is the one you can't defend.

08 — FAQ

Questions buyers actually ask

Who is Vetori for? ▾
The person who owns the board deck at a venture-backed startup — Chief of Staff, Head of Finance, VP Finance, or BizOps. If you rebuild the board update from spreadsheets every cycle, Vetori is for you.
Can the board interact with the report? ▾
Yes. Board members can play the narrated recap OR take over and explore — filter, search, drill into a row, change a dimension — on the same link, with no account and no BI seat.
How is this different from Visible or Foundersuite? ▾
Visible and Foundersuite are KPI dashboards and investor-email tools — you still build the actual report. Vetori writes the board report itself from your data: the charts, the framing, the narrative. Then your board can drill into any number.
How is this different from Gamma or PowerPoint? ▾
Gamma and PowerPoint make pretty slides, but you paste the numbers in by hand and they can be wrong. Vetori generates the report from your spreadsheet, so every figure is computed from your data and one click from the query behind it. And it's a live link, not a static file — your board can explore it.
What data formats do you support? ▾
Currently CSV. Vetori auto-detects columns, types, and coordinates. Native data-warehouse connectors are on the roadmap.
Are AI-authored stories live yet? ▾
Yes. AI-authored stories ship in the closed beta. Audio narration over the playback is the next item on the roadmap.
What if the AI gets a number wrong? ▾
It can't invent numbers. Every figure is computed by a query against your spreadsheet, and any number in the report is one click from the exact query that produced it. You review and approve every narrative before it ships, with an audit trail of versions.
Do board members need an account? ▾
No. Board members open the report through a dedicated viewer flow — magic-link or password-protected — without needing an authoring account or a BI seat.
Can we match the brand for each report? ▾
Yes. Custom subdomain, tunable colors, paid plans remove Vetori branding entirely — so the board report looks like it came straight from your company.
