01 — The problem

Your reporting tool stops where the board meeting starts.

Most of the reporting cycle goes to wrangling and reformatting, not advising. And the part clients actually pay for — the narrative, in the boardroom — still gets built by hand.

The deck rebuild

10–40 hours a quarter, rebuilt by hand.

Reporting tools stop at a static PDF. The pack the board actually sees gets reassembled in Slides every cycle — same charts, new numbers, copy-paste in between. That's where traceability dies.

AI trust

86% of CFOs have caught AI inventing numbers.

Only 14% completely trust AI outputs (Wakefield/Maximor, Jan 2026). Your firm's product is its credibility — you can't sign prose you can't verify.

The silent PDF

Did they even read it?

The pack goes out as an attachment and lands in silence. No questions, no signal — no way to know whether the founder ever opened it.

02 — The difference

Every number survives being clicked.

Click any figure in a Vetori pack and the inspector opens: the query, the SQL, the rows it returned. Not buried in a footnote — in the pack itself, for everyone you invite. When your client's board member checks a number, it holds.

Deck generators write numbers as text — in independent testing, 41% of the statistics in Copilot-generated decks were fabricated (Octigen). Vetori's numbers aren't text. They're query results.

06 — FAQ

Questions firms actually ask

What if the AI gets a number wrong? ▾
It can't invent one. Every figure in the pack is computed by a query against your client's data, and the query is one click away in the pack itself. Nothing is shared before you review and approve it.
What does my client's board see? ▾
A link, under your firm's brand. No account, no seat, nothing to learn. They read the narrated pack, click into any number, and verify it themselves.
What data does it work with? ▾
Your exports from QuickBooks, Xero, and Stripe — CSV today. Vetori detects fields, types, and time periods on its own.
We already use a reporting tool. Why this? ▾
Reporting tools stop at the static report — the actual board pack still gets rebuilt by hand each cycle, and a PDF can't answer "where did this number come from?". Vetori is the board-meeting artifact itself: narrated, verifiable, always current.
How is this different from Gamma or Copilot decks? ▾
Deck generators write numbers as generated text — in independent testing, 41% of statistics in Copilot-generated decks were fabricated (Octigen). Vetori's numbers are query results, computed from your client's data, with the query one click away.
What does "founding firm" involve? ▾
We build your first client pack with you, on your client's real data. You get a direct line to the founders, a roadmap shaped around your client types, and founding pricing locked in. There's no public pricing yet.
Is my client's data private? ▾
Private by default. You invite the founder and board members individually, and you can revoke access anytime. Each pack lives under your firm's brand on your domain.
